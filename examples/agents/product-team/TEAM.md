---
name: Product team
description: An orchestrator who coordinates planning, building, design and quality. Each member gets a name when it is installed; untick whoever you do not need.
---

The orchestrator takes the owner's requests and gives each task to one owner; the others are asked when a change
needs them: a short design check, one review at the end, a test pass. Work passes between them as tasks, not chats; a
quick question is a `delegate`. At the end, each one creates the follow-ups of its own role and proposes the rest (QA,
reviews, another area's work) for the owner to accept.

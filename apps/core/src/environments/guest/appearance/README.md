# APX desktop appearance

Dark wallpaper: `apx-wallpaper-v1.png`. Light counterpart: `apx-wallpaper-light-v1.png`. The APX mark in `apps/web/src/assets/logo.png` is the brand reference. Keep the left side quiet for desktop icons and the emblem visible on the right.

Palette: graphite/forest background, emerald `#1a9460`, mint `#3ec98b`, pale text `#e1f4e8`. GTK uses its built-in Adwaita light/dark bases, matching APX. The `.gtk` files contain GTK-specific CSS and are installed as `gtk.css`. Window frames reuse the guest's installed Default XPM artwork and its accompanying license.

## Wallpaper brief

Transform the supplied APX logo into a polished default desktop wallpaper for the APX agent computer. Landscape 16:10. Preserve the recognizable four-arrow emblem and central dot. Recolor emerald and mint rather than neon yellow. Extremely dark graphite and deep forest-green background (`#0b1512`), quiet sculptural flowing ribbons or folded translucent green material sweeping diagonally across the right half, subtle topographic depth, soft volumetric light, restrained modern operating system aesthetic. Place the emblem slightly right of center, approximately 12 percent of the width, with the exact wordmark “APX” beneath it. Keep the left half quiet and dark with empty space for icons. No UI, windows, computer mockup, borders, slogans or other text. Fill the canvas with an actual calm desktop wallpaper.

## Light counterpart brief

Preserve the original composition, APX emblem and lettering, flowing ribbons and quiet left side. Change only lighting and palette: airy warm-white ivory `#f4f7f2`, pale mint ribbons and emerald `#1a9460`, soft diffuse daylight, delicate shadows and dark emerald lettering. Keep the same landscape aspect ratio, without UI, borders or extra text.

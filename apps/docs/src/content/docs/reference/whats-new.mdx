When APX starts on a newer version, your super-agent opens a chat with what changed and offers the new things that
fit how you use it (see [Updates](/start/install/#updates)). This page keeps that list for later, with a link to the page
that explains each part. Fixes that only make something work as it already should are left out, and a release that
only fixed things says so in one line. 2.13.0 was never published: its work shipped in 2.13.1.

## 2.17.2

- Research fills itself in while you chat. Mention an interest, a project to follow, a public page or a place you
  care about, and your super-agent adds it to your research; a small **Research updated** line under its answer opens
  to what it added. See [Contextual research](/office/contextual-research/).
- Research tells you on its own: something that can't wait right away, the rest in one short summary at 8 in the
  morning; or, if you prefer, inside a routine you choose. Interests stay short search terms, and you can keep several
  places (cities or regions) to hear about news and events near there. See
  [Contextual research](/office/contextual-research/#how-you-hear-about-findings).
- Quiet work in a chat shows as small lines instead of steps: **Saved to memory**, **Research updated** and
  **Chat summarized**. See [Talking to an agent](/office/chat/#talking-to-an-agent).
- **Settings › Review and improvements** lists past reviews: when each ran, how many chats it read, and what became
  of every improvement it suggested. See [History](/office/secretary/#history).
- Settings that save with a button (Review and improvements, the router, shared image defaults, email signatures)
  keep your changes in a bar at the bottom of the screen, with **Discard** and **Save changes**; ⌘/Ctrl+S saves too.
  See [The router](/concepts/models/#the-router).

## 2.17.1

- Contextual research, off until you turn it on: APX looks at public sources in the context of your projects and
  interests and adds one supported finding to a report you already get. **Settings › Research** has the switch, the
  setup and your reports, with a bar at the bottom to save or discard changes. See
  [Contextual research](/office/contextual-research/).

## 2.17.0

### Agents

- Your agents work across all your projects and chats, and say where they acted. A role or a `tools:` list says what
  an agent is good at and uses first; it no longer makes it refuse your work. See
  [What your agents can reach](/concepts/agents/#what-your-agents-can-reach).
- Big jobs run as workflows: several agents at once, each result checked before the next stage, on one card you can
  stop and resume. See [Workflows](/concepts/agents/#workflows).
- A delegated agent that hits its step or time limit keeps going in the same conversation while it makes progress,
  and the chat shows a quiet notice with what it has done so far. Teammates asked from any of a task's chats show
  under that task in the Inbox. See [Delegation](/concepts/agents/#delegation) and
  [Tasks](/office/tasks/#attachments-and-task-chat-actions).
- A Claude Code or Codex session an agent opens shows as that engine, named after its task, with a **Started by**
  chip back to the chat that asked. See [Sessions your agent starts](/office/code/#sessions-your-agent-starts).
- Your super-agent makes the settings changes you ask for in Auto without a card each time: memory's search by
  meaning, models, voice, transcription, a media service, and which tools another agent may use. Deleting for good,
  keys and who reaches you still wait for you. See [Change settings from chat](/office/secretary/#change-settings-from-chat)
  and [Deleting](/concepts/autonomy/#deleting).

### Chats and follow-ups

- Ask an agent to come back to the same chat once, at a time or when a task finishes ("When this task finishes,
  check its result"). The receipt has **Cancel**, and the result lands in that chat. See
  [Return to this chat once](/office/routines/#return-to-this-chat-once).
- Files and changed files open in tabs in the side panel. See [The panels you'll use](/office/code/#the-panels-youll-use).

### Models

- **Settings › Helper model** shows what the small background model does and lets you pick it with backups in order.
  See [The helper model](/concepts/models/#the-helper-model).
- When one model fails for a passing reason and the rest are at their limit, the turn asks the list again before it
  waits for a limit to lift. See [An agent's own fallbacks](/concepts/models/#an-agents-own-fallbacks).

### Projects

- A project's agents, notes and skills live in `.apx/`. APX moves an older `.apc/` folder there by itself, at start and
  when you add a project. See [Projects from earlier versions](/concepts/projects/#projects-from-earlier-versions-apc).

### Browser

- At a sign-in page your agent finds the username and password you saved for that site, or asks you to type them on
  a card it never sees. See [Signing in with your saved passwords](/office/browser/#signing-in-with-your-saved-passwords).
- Agents can save browser work that worked as a recipe and repeat it later in one step, attaching files on the way.
  See [Repeating a job: recipes](/office/browser/#repeating-a-job-recipes).

### Contacts

- An agent you assign to a contact can answer from its project's notes when you allow it, and, with project reading
  on for that person, read its project for them. When its reading isn't enough, it asks your super-agent, and you can open that
  consultation from the contact's chat. See [Reading the assigned agent's project](/channels/overview/#reading-the-assigned-agents-project).

### Extensions

- Extensions that support notices can check in the background and wake the agent you choose when something changes.
  See [Background observations](/extend/extensions/#background-observations).
- The Marketplace shows who made each extension, apart from APX's review badge. See
  [Find them in the Marketplace](/extend/extensions/#find-them-in-the-marketplace).

### Phone

- **Tasks** lists what needs you first. A long press on **Chats**, **Tasks** or **Routines** marks that tab read, and on
  **Chats** it also offers **See archived**. A reply cut by a dropped connection fills in when the app reconnects. See [Navigate the Android app](/devices/phone/#navigate-the-android-app).

## 2.16.1

- Your agent can work with Google spreadsheets through your Drive connection: list tabs, read cells and create
  spreadsheets, with cell changes following your confirmation preferences. See [Google](/extend/integrations/#google).
- When a Google API is turned off, the message says which one and who can turn it on. The rest are fixes, mostly for
  adding and updating extensions.

## 2.16.0

- The Marketplace: APX's catalog, the extensions that come with APX and your own catalogs in one place, each saying
  what it brings, who makes it and what it can reach. Adding, connecting and pairing a device happen in one box. See
  [Extensions](/extend/extensions/#find-them-in-the-marketplace).
- Share a widget, app or page with a public link anyone can open without APX. You approve exactly what goes public
  and can stop sharing it at any time. See [Publish a public copy](/office/artifacts/#publish-a-public-copy).
- An optional APX account: sign in with Google in **Settings › Account** to get your APX ID. Your projects and chats
  stay on your computer. See [Your APX account](/start/account/).
- Contacts can book appointments on your Google Calendar, at a free time inside your attention hours. See
  [Appointments](/channels/overview/#appointments).
- Move an agent to another project with its tasks and chosen files, after a review, without making a copy. See
  [Move an agent and its work to another project](/concepts/agent-moves/).
- APX can run with `docker compose up`, from an image each release publishes. See [Install](/start/install/).

## 2.15.3

- Calls can use the speech and transcription services you choose (Gemini, ElevenLabs or an OpenAI-compatible
  server), on Windows, Linux or macOS. See [Choose services for calls](/office/calls/#choose-services-for-calls).

## 2.15.1

## 2.15.0

- APX has its own voice, in Spanish or English, and you can call any agent: work that takes time keeps going during
  the call, and you hear the answer when it's ready. See [Calls and APX's voice](/office/calls/).
- Group chats: you and two to five agents in one chat; @name asks one of them. See
  [Group chats](/office/chat/#group-chats).
- A simple panel for a personal assistant, with **Simple** and **Advanced** at the top to switch, and OpenRouter
  connects by signing in, starting on its free models. See [Personal assistant](/start/first-steps/#personal-assistant).
- Results to compare (stays, flights, products) can come as cards inside an answer, on the panel and the phone. See
  [Tables, code, pages and diagrams in answers](/office/chat/#tables-code-pages-and-diagrams-in-answers).
- The chat shows what the agent is thinking before it answers. See [Talking to an agent](/office/chat/#talking-to-an-agent).

## 2.14.3

- A routine runs where its agent works, with that agent's tools (your Chrome included), and each routine has its own
  **Permissions**. See [Where it runs and what it may do](/office/routines/#where-it-runs-and-what-it-may-do).
- Say "let's go on in Telegram" and your agent writes there and carries on; results of work it handed off reach you
  where you last wrote. See [Where your messages land](/channels/overview/#where-your-messages-land).
- Gemini 4 Argon and GPT-6 Astra show up in the model picker. See [Model launch notices](/concepts/models/#model-launch-notices).
- The welcome shows what's new as short clips, one per feature.

## 2.14.2

Fixes only: updating from the 2.13.4 hotfix repairs the database it left behind.

## 2.14.1

Fixes only: updating from the 2.13.4 hotfix no longer stops on indexes added by hand. See
[Updating from the v2.13.4 hotfix](/start/install/#updating-from-the-v2134-hotfix).

## 2.14.0

- Several accounts of the same chat app (two WhatsApp numbers, two bots), each with who answers you and who answers
  your contacts, and an agent of its own for one contact or one room. See
  [Who is on an account](/channels/overview/#who-is-on-an-account).
- WhatsApp groups: pick which groups APX reads and when your agent answers in each. See [Groups](/channels/whatsapp/#groups).
- Ask in a Discord room or a group for something that needs real work and your agent does it from Home; the result
  reaches you in private. See [Server rooms](/channels/discord-slack/#server-rooms).
- Received mail shows as mail, pictures hidden until you ask, and you can stop reading a sender or a domain. See
  [Reading received mail](/channels/email/#reading-received-mail).
- Subprojects: each has its own folder, chats and memory, and the project's team works in all of them. See
  [Subprojects](/concepts/projects/#subprojects).
- **Side question** in the composer's + menu asks your agent something about the chat without adding to it. See
  [Side question](/office/chat/#side-question).
- Review and improvements: APX reviews new activity and suggests small improvements as proposals. See
  [Review and improvements](/office/secretary/#review-and-improvements).
- A project's Memory lists each note in a short row, and your super-agent can go through them with you. See
  [See, confirm and undo](/concepts/memory/#see-confirm-and-undo).
- Changed agents leave a review notice, and the agent on a task carries it to the end. See
  [Review agents after changes](/concepts/agents/#review-agents-after-changes).
- An MCP connection can move between global and one project, **All allowed** covers tools a server adds later, and a
  connection can be edited from a link in the chat. See [Global and project servers](/extend/mcp/#global-and-project-servers).
- A calendar reminder you don't need can be turned off from the reminder itself, for that event and its series. See
  [Google](/extend/integrations/#google).

## 2.13.4

Fixes only: shell commands refuse hidden null characters, and a command that fails to start no longer stays open.

## 2.13.3

- What you ask your super-agent for follows its permission mode. The rest are fixes: a ChatGPT plan no longer refuses
  a chat after text cut mid-emoji, and APX waits for your Chrome's extension before giving up. See
  [Autonomy and safety](/concepts/autonomy/).

## 2.13.2

- On the phone: messages waiting for the agent can be sent now, edited, moved or taken back, tasks show what you
  asked in your words, and More warns when the computer's disk is almost full. See
  [Navigate the Android app](/devices/phone/#navigate-the-android-app).

## 2.13.1

- Tasks have a goal, keep what you asked as you wrote it, and show the agent's plan step by step; agents comment
  less and you can group related tasks under one parent. See [Goal, what you asked and the plan](/office/tasks/#goal-what-you-asked-and-the-plan).
- Write while your agent works: it reads you at its next step, or waits in line until it finishes. See
  [Writing while it works](/office/chat/#writing-while-it-works).
- Agents can work in your own Chrome, where you are already signed in, from APX's side panel in Chrome. See
  [In your own Chrome](/office/browser/#in-your-own-chrome).
- Agents fill a saved password, username or authenticator code into a sign-in form without seeing it. See
  [Signing in with your saved passwords](/office/browser/#signing-in-with-your-saved-passwords).
- Ask for a web page and it shows as one, with **Edit** to point at a part and change it. See
  [Web pages](/office/artifacts/#web-pages).
- Each agent can have up to five fallback models. See [An agent's own fallbacks](/concepts/models/#an-agents-own-fallbacks).
- Models from an app running on this computer (Ollama, LM Studio) are ready to pick. See
  [Models on this computer](/concepts/models/#models-on-this-computer).
- Ask your agent to open a Claude Code or Codex session in a project. See
  [Sessions your agent starts](/office/code/#sessions-your-agent-starts).
- **Settings › Move or back up** saves your setup as one file to bring to another computer, and a Hermes setup can
  come in too. See [Move or back up](/start/move-or-back-up/) and [Moving from Hermes](/start/from-hermes/).
- **Settings › Storage** shows what APX keeps on disk and frees space, and compacts the chats' file. See
  [The disk is almost full](/reference/troubleshooting/#the-disk-is-almost-full).
- Emails your agent writes show as cards you send from Gmail or an Email channel. See
  [Send a draft from the chat](/office/email/#send-a-draft-from-the-chat).
- A small cursor shows what each agent is about to do on your Mac, and quick chat can start with any agent. See
  [Native agent cursor](/devices/desktop/#native-agent-cursor).

## 2.12.0

  AI model steps, project tables, and a test with one real event. Your super-agent can draw them for you. See
  [Flows](/office/routines/#flows).
- A public address for Flows, LINE, Teams and the widgets you publish, while your panel and files stay private. See
  [A public address for Teams and LINE](/channels/overview/#a-public-address-for-teams-and-line).
- Agents can run a script in APX's browser after you approve it, once, for a tab or for one turn. See
  [Approved JavaScript execution](/office/browser/#approved-javascript-execution).
- Google Calendar can remind you before each event, and Gmail's mail check runs as often as you choose. See
  [Google](/extend/integrations/#google).
- The **?** at the foot of the sidebar keeps what each release brought. See [What's new and help](/start/first-steps/#whats-new-and-help).
- Agents keep going until they finish what you asked, with no turn or token limit by default. See
  [Delegation](/concepts/agents/#delegation).
- **Files** lists everything shared in chats and leads back to its message. See
  [Everything shared, in Files](/office/chat/#everything-shared-in-files).
- Several mailboxes, each with its own signature, and a text size you choose in **Settings › Appearance**. See
  [Several mailboxes and signatures](/channels/email/#several-mailboxes-and-signatures) and
  [Languages and themes](/reference/configuration/#languages-and-themes).

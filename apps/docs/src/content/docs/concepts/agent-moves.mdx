A move keeps an agent's identity and instructions. It is not a clone and does not rewrite the agent's role.
The source and destination must already be registered projects with available folders.

## Review before moving

Open the agent's menu in its project's **Agents** tab and choose **Move to another project**.
Choose a destination and browse the source folder to select the files or folders that belong with this work.
Review the inventory, including completed and shared tasks, related chats, memory, attachments and widgets.
Confirm the destination and affected work before applying the move.

The inventory follows task relationships and recorded participation, not the meaning of every message or file.
Shared tasks move too; their comments, history and attribution stay intact. Other agents do not move with them.
The review identifies agents detached from the moved work and the team already in the destination.
Agent hierarchy is detached where necessary so a source-project parent or child does not silently cross projects.

Only selected source work files move. Relevant widget bundles and sources are included separately.
Original studies and other files already in the destination are not overwritten or moved again.
Linked paths, nested projects, credential files, collisions and oversized selections are refused.
Select fewer than 10,000 files and no more than 256 MiB in total.

## Connections, routines and permissions

Moved routines are paused. Saved tool and folder grants are retired, not copied into the new project.
Review the destination's connections and variables before restarting work: a variable name can resolve to a different
value there. APX does not copy secret values or source-project connection definitions into the destination.

Running work in the source, active background work, connected/group chats, published widgets and Flow bindings can
prevent a move. Resolve these dependencies and request a new preview. Project-local skills require separate review;
the move currently refuses them rather than silently losing their definitions.
Projects that inherit context from one another cannot be used to establish this separation.

## What separation means

The moved work belongs to the destination. Former source teammates lose participation in its chats and tasks;
APX checks chat searches, attachment reads and native folder access against the new scope. A destination protected
by a move cannot exchange delegated work with another project's agents; the owner's assistant remains able to help.
Moved notes are scoped to the destination, including formerly global notes attached to the selected work.
Persisted model memory snapshots are rechecked so an old snapshot cannot restore a moved note to the source's context.

This is not an erasure of history or an operating-system sandbox. The owner's chats, author attribution and unrelated
source conversations may retain earlier mentions or quotations. Existing copies, exports, backups, external services,
broad shell access and independently authorized integrations are not retroactively revoked or scrubbed.
Do not treat a move as a guarantee that every historical mention has disappeared.

## Recover safely

The core keeps a private journal and backups under `APX_HOME/agent-moves/<move-id>`.
These may contain sensitive work; keep them out of repositories and shared folders. Recovery material is retained,
not automatically erased after success. Protect and back up the APX home accordingly.

A private manifest is saved before staging begins, so an interrupted preparation can be retired without complete file journals or changes to the originals.
On restart, APX attempts to finish a committed move or restore an interrupted one. Affected work stays blocked when
recovery cannot complete. Recovery refuses changed files or newer related database work instead of overwriting them.
This journal handles interrupted processes; it does not replace a backup against disk failure or guarantee survival
of every power-loss scenario.

For the supported preview/apply/recover commands, see the [CLI reference](/reference/cli/).
Ask the super-agent to prepare a preview or recover a move; applying or recovering requires the owner's approval,
even when the agent normally works in Total mode. Do not edit SQLite or the recovery journal by hand.

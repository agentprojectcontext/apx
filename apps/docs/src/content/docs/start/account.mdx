---
title: Your APX account
description: Sign in with Google, find your APX ID and choose an optional username.
---

An APX account is optional. Your projects, agents and local conversations keep working without signing in.
Signing in does not upload or synchronize those projects or conversations, and does not connect Gmail,
Google Calendar or Google Drive. Connect those separately in **Resources › Integrations**.

## Sign in

1. Open **Settings › Account**.
2. In the card at the top (you, the owner of this install), choose **Sign in with Google**.
3. Finish signing in in your browser. The tab shows APX joined to Google while it connects; it says
   **Done, you’re signed in as** your name only once APX has saved your session and read it back. Choose
   **Close tab** (or close it yourself if your browser does not let the page do it) and return to APX: the panel
   notices on its own. If it says **Could not sign in**, the reason is under it and **Try again** starts over.
4. The same card now shows your photo, your name and your eight-character APX ID, with **Sign out**.

If your name in APX was still the default (**You**), it takes your Google name. If you had typed another one, APX
shows both and asks which one stays; nothing else in your profile (about you, language, time zone) changes.

**Name** is who you are; **How to call you** is how your agents talk to you and how the panel names you (empty: by
your name). Signing in never changes how you asked to be called.

Keep APX running while you sign in. A sign-in expires after ten minutes; if it expires or you restart APX,
start again from Settings. The browser and the core must run on the same computer for this sign-in flow.
If the browser blocks the sign-in window, allow pop-ups for your APX address and try again. Cancelling in Google
ends that attempt; you can start again from APX. If your saved session is revoked, APX removes it and asks you to
sign in again. Temporary connection errors keep your saved session so you can retry. If APX still keeps a session
but can no longer read it on this computer, your account card says so with **Sign in with Google** beside it;
signing in again replaces it.

## Choose a username

Your username is optional and unique. Use 3–24 lowercase letters, numbers or underscores, starting with a
letter. You can type a leading `@`; APX removes it when saving. Choose **Save**. If a name is already taken,
choose another one. Clear the field and save to remove your username. Changing your username does not
change your APX ID.

The ID uses Crockford base32, without the letters I, L, O or U. Your account profile is private; creating
an account does not publish anything.

## Sign out

Choose **Sign out** on your account card. APX removes the saved session from this core. Your cloud account
and profile remain, and your local work is not deleted. Other cores signed in to the same account stay signed in.

If sign-in is not set up yet, you can keep using APX without an account. A self-hosted build
needs its Firebase and Google client configuration; see the repository's `firebase/README.md`.

The first time APX opens, a short wizard sets up your crew. It takes a few minutes, everything in it can be changed
later in Settings, and you can open it again from the account menu › **First-run setup**.

## The setup wizard

<Shot name="wizard-welcome" alt="The first-run setup on its welcome step, with the choices Start from scratch and Import from APX v1" caption="Welcome: start fresh or bring your v1 crew" />

<Steps>

1. **Welcome.** Choose **Start from scratch**. If APX finds an APX v1 on this computer, it also offers
   **Import from APX v1**, with how many projects it found. Importing adds a step where you pick what to bring
   (see [Coming from v1](/start/from-v1/)).

2. **Super-agent.** This is Home, your secretary: it coordinates the rest and answers your channels. Give it a
   **Name** and pick its blob, one of 35 faces. It shows up beside the form as you choose.

   <Shot name="wizard-agent" alt="The agent step: a name field, a grid of blob faces and the chosen blob shown large on the right" />

3. **Engines.** Connect your models: sign in with your ChatGPT plan, or **Add API key** for
   Gemini, Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter and others. Local engines such as Ollama need no key. Then order the
   **Model chain** with the arrows: the first model is the default, and if it fails the next one takes over.

4. **Memory.** Choose **Search by words** (notes found by the words they share with your message), **Search by
   meaning** (the same, plus notes that say it differently; needs an embedding engine like Ollama, Gemini or OpenAI)
   or **No memory**. Two switches below: pick skills by meaning for each message, and keep long chats small with
   summaries.

5. **Channels.** Optional. Paste a Telegram bot token from @BotFather and press **Connect**, or link WhatsApp with
   a QR as you would WhatsApp Web. Discord and Slack are set up later in Settings › Channels.

6. **Projects.** Your base project is always there and has no folder of its own; Home and your global agents live
   in it. You can rename it, add a folder now with **With a folder**, or skip with **Not now**. Press
   **Create crew**.

7. **Done.** Your crew is ready. Press **Open APX**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
No desktop? On a server, `apx setup --headless` does the same job from the terminal, and `apx wizard` opens a guided
console for status, devices, Tailscale, pairing and updates.
</Aside>

## Pick a model

You chose a default model chain in the wizard. You can change it at any time:

- **Settings › Engines and models** lists every engine with its models and lets you add keys or sign in.
- **Settings › Router** sets rules that pick a model per turn (images, long messages, a channel, keywords) and the
  fallback chain.
- In a chat, the model picker in the composer changes the model for that chat only, and `/model` does the same from
  the keyboard.

From the terminal, `apx models` lists every engine and its models, and `apx models default` sets the install's
default. More in [Models](/concepts/models/).

## Say hi to Home

Home's chat is in the **Inbox**. Write in the composer ("Message …" with your secretary's name) and press
Enter.

<Shot name="home-hero" alt="The Inbox with Home's chat open and a short exchange with the secretary" />

Try something real:

- "What can you do for me?"
- "Remind me to call Northwind about the invoice on Friday at 10."
- "Open the Acme Store site and tell me what the home page says."

Home's chat is one conversation everywhere: when you connect Telegram or WhatsApp, the messages you send there land
here too.

## Add a project

A project is a folder your agents work in: a repo, a shop, a company folder. In the sidebar, press **New project**.
The **Add a project** dialog asks **Where it lives**:

- **In a folder**: pick a folder like `~/work/acme`. If it has no `AGENTS.md`, APX writes one naming the project.
- **No folder**: the project is made inside APX's own folder.

Or from the terminal:

<Terminal command="apx projects register ~/work/acme" />

The project shows up in the sidebar with its tabs: Overview, Chats, Agents, Tasks, Routines, Files, Memory and
Resources. Add agents in the **Agents** tab with **Agent**, or bring one from a template with **Import**. More in [Projects](/concepts/projects/).

## Ask from the terminal

`apx chat` is the one way to talk to an agent from the terminal. With no options it writes to Home:

<Terminal command={`apx chat "What's still open in Acme Store?"`}>{`Two tasks are open in Acme Store: Tomás is fixing the checkout bug,
and Vera's newsletter is waiting for your review.
— completed · thread home`}</Terminal>

Talk to a specific agent with `--agent`, start a fresh chat with `--new`, or open a code thread in the folder you're
in with `--code`:

```bash
apx chat "Draft the release notes" --agent "Lucía"
apx chat "Why does the cart test fail?" --code --cwd ~/work/acme
```

Everything you write from the terminal shows up in the app, and the other way around.

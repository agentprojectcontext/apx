The first time APX opens, a short wizard sets it up. Everything in it can be changed later in Settings, and in the
full panel you can open it again from the account menu › **First-run setup**.

## The setup wizard

<Shot name="wizard-welcome" alt="The first-run setup on its welcome step, with the choices Personal assistant and Projects and team" caption="Welcome: pick how you will use APX" />

The first screen asks **How will you use APX?**:

- **Personal assistant** (the simple panel): one agent that helps you every day with your mail, Telegram, calls and
  reminders. A few steps, and a panel without projects.
- **Projects and team** (the full panel): agents for each project, tasks, flows and every setting. The steps below
  under [Projects and team](#projects-and-team).

You can switch between them any time with **Simple** and **Advanced** at the top of the panel, or ask your
super-agent to do it.

### Personal assistant

<Steps>

1. **Its brain.** Click **ChatGPT** to sign in with your Plus or Pro plan, or **OpenRouter** to approve APX in your
   OpenRouter account (it opens in a window of its own and starts on OpenRouter's free models). One is enough; with
   both connected, the next step asks which goes first. A model app on this computer, such as Ollama, stays as the
   backup without asking. **More options** lists what is connected and **Add provider** shows every kind as a card.

2. **Its name and face.** Pick one of three names (the die offers three others) or write your own, and pick its face.

3. **Channels.** Optional: link WhatsApp with a QR, connect Gmail, or paste a [Telegram](/channels/telegram/) bot
   token. **Skip for now** leaves them for later.

4. **Meet.** While APX gets things ready on its own (memory that searches by meaning when Ollama is here, its own
   voice for voice notes and calls where this computer can run it, images with ChatGPT), your super-agent asks what
   to call you, what you do, how to talk to you and what to help with first, each with answers to pick. Press **Enter
   APX** whenever you want: the downloads go on.

</Steps>

On the simple panel your super-agent works on its own: it does not create projects or other agents. Ask it for
project work and it offers to switch to the full panel.

### Projects and team

<Steps>

1. **Welcome.** Choose **Projects and team**. If APX finds an APX v1 on this computer, it also offers
   **Bring APX v1**, with how many projects it found. Importing adds a step where you pick what to bring
   (see [Coming from v1](/start/from-v1/)).

2. **Super-agent.** This is Home, your secretary: it coordinates the rest and answers your channels. Give it a
   **Name** and pick its blob, one of 35 faces. It shows up beside the form as you choose.

3. **Engines.** Connect your models: sign in with your ChatGPT plan, or add a key for
   Gemini, Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter and others. Local engines such as Ollama need no key. Then order the
   **Model chain** with the arrows: the first model is the default, and if it fails the next one takes over.

4. **Memory.** Choose **Search by words** (notes found by the words they share with your message), **Search by
   meaning** (the same, plus notes that say it differently; needs an embedding engine like Ollama, Gemini or OpenAI)
   or **No memory**. Two switches below: pick skills by meaning for each message, and keep long chats small with
   summaries.

5. **Channels.** Optional. Paste a [Telegram](/channels/telegram/) bot token from @BotFather and press **Connect**, or link WhatsApp with
   a QR as you would WhatsApp Web. Discord and Slack are set up later in Channels.

6. **Projects.** Your base project is always there and has no folder of its own; Home and your global agents live
   in it. You can rename it, add a folder now with **With a folder**, or skip with **Not now**. Press
   **Create crew**.

7. **Done.** Your crew is ready. Press **Open APX**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
No desktop? On a server, `apx setup --headless` does the same job from the terminal, and `apx wizard` opens a guided
console for status, devices, Tailscale, pairing and updates.
</Aside>

## Pick a model

You chose a default model chain in the wizard. You can change it at any time:

- **Settings › Engines and models** lists every engine with its models and lets you add keys or sign in.
- **Settings › Router** sets rules that pick a model per turn (images, long messages, a channel, keywords) and the
  fallback chain.
- In a chat, the model picker in the composer changes the model for that chat only, and `/model` does the same from
  the keyboard.

From the terminal, `apx models` lists every engine and its models, and `apx models default` sets the install's
default. More in [Models](/concepts/models/).

## Say hi to Home

Home's chat is in the **Inbox**. Write in the composer ("Message …" with your secretary's name) and press
Enter.

<Shot name="home-hero" alt="The Inbox with Home's chat open and a short exchange with the secretary" />

Try something real:

- "What can you do for me?"
- "Remind me to call Northwind about the invoice on Friday at 10."
- "Open the Acme Store site and tell me what the home page says."

Home's chat is one conversation everywhere: when you connect Telegram or WhatsApp, the messages you send there land
here too.

## Add a project

A project is a folder your agents work in: a repo, a shop, a company folder. In the sidebar, press **New project**.
The **Add a project** dialog asks **Where it lives**:

- **In a folder**: pick a folder like `~/work/acme`. If it has no `AGENTS.md`, APX writes one naming the project.
- **No folder**: the project is made inside APX's own folder.

Or from the terminal:

<Terminal command="apx projects register ~/work/acme" />

The project shows up in the sidebar with its tabs: Overview, Chats, Agents, Tasks, Routines, Files, Memory and
Resources. Add agents in the **Agents** tab with **Agent**, or bring one from a template with **Import**. More in [Projects](/concepts/projects/).

## Ask from the terminal

`apx chat` is the one way to talk to an agent from the terminal. With no options it writes to Home:

<Terminal command={`apx chat "What's still open in Acme Store?"`}>{`Two tasks are open in Acme Store: Tomás is fixing the checkout bug,
and Vera's newsletter is waiting for your review.
— completed · thread home`}</Terminal>

Talk to a specific agent with `--agent`, start a fresh chat with `--new`, or open a code thread in the folder you're
in with `--code`:

```bash
apx chat "Draft the release notes" --agent "Lucía"
apx chat "Why does the cart test fail?" --code --cwd ~/work/acme
```

Everything you write from the terminal shows up in the app, and the other way around.

## Fold the sidebar

The sidebar's right edge folds it into a column of icons: click the edge, or drag it most of the way to the left. Click
it again, or drag it back out, to unfold it at the width you had. Dragging it also sets that width. Folded, each
section keeps its **+** as its first icon.

The sidebar starts compact: Projects, External sessions, Channels and Apps are one row each, and resting the pointer on
a row (or clicking it) opens its items beside it. A dot on its icon says something there waits to be read or is
working. To see each section as a card with its rows always open, turn off **Compact sidebar**
in Settings › Appearance or in the menu under your name. In a window too short for the cards it stays compact.

Right click on the sidebar to choose which views (Inbox, Tasks, Flows…) and sections (Projects, Channels…) it shows,
and to turn compact on or off. Press and drag a view or a section to move it; the order stays on this device.

## What's new and help

The **?** at the foot of the sidebar shows what the last releases brought. Pick one to read what it lets you do and ask
your super-agent to set it up for you, or open **All the news** to go through every release. Each one you have not
opened yet carries a green dot, and so does the **?** while any is left; opening it clears its dot. When there are more
than the menu has room for, **All the news** says how many, and opening it marks them all as seen. The same menu has the
guides, the Discord and a way to send feedback. When APX updates, your super-agent tells you the one thing that matters
most in a line; the rest waits in that menu.

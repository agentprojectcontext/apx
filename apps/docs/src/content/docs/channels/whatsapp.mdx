Connect WhatsApp to APX and reach your agent from a private chat. APX links as a WhatsApp linked device using a
QR code. It does not use the WhatsApp Business API and does not require creating a bot account.

APX must be running on your computer or your own server. The WhatsApp connection lets you reach that installation
from your phone. Before you start, [install APX](/start/install/) and have the phone with the number you want to
link ready. Choose a separate line for your agent or your own **Message yourself** chat, then scan the QR.

## Two ways to use it

**A line of its own** (the default). A second number, used only by your agent. You write to it from your own phone,
like you'd write to an assistant. This is the setup most people want: your agent has its own chats, and yours stay
yours.

**Your own number.** Turn on **This line is my own phone** and you talk to your agent in WhatsApp's
"Message yourself" chat. What you type to other people on your phone stays yours: APX never answers in your name
there.

You can switch later with the same toggle in the channel's settings.

## Link it

<Steps>

1. Under **Channels** in the sidebar, open **Manage channels**, press **New channel** and pick **WhatsApp**. Give it a name if you like
   (Sales, Personal…).

2. Press **Connect**. A QR shows up; it changes every few seconds and the page keeps it fresh.

3. On the phone that has the number: **WhatsApp › Settings › Linked devices › Link a device**, and point it at the
   code.

4. Once it is connected, set **This line is my own phone** if you linked your own number, choose **Who answers**, and
   press **Create channel**.

</Steps>

The status turns to **Connected**. WhatsApp support loads the first time you link it, so that first connection can
take a moment.

## Link your phone to the line

On a line of its own, APX doesn't know which number is you until you tell it. Until then, what you write to the line
is a stranger's. Two ways, under **Your phone**:

- **Write to the line** from your own WhatsApp. The request shows up in the list; tap **It's my number**. WhatsApp
  shows the number, so no code is needed.
- **Add my number**: type it with the country code (`+1 555 0100`) and press **Save**.

Once linked, your messages go to **Home**, or to a chat of their own if you set **Your messages** to **Own chat**.

On your own number there's nothing to link: whoever writes in "Message yourself" is you.

## Chats with contacts

APX reads one-to-one chats, and the [groups you list](#groups). Broadcasts and WhatsApp channels are ignored. Voice notes in private chats are
transcribed (see [Voice notes](/channels/overview/#voice-notes)), and options the agent offers you arrive as a
numbered list: answer with the number. Photos, videos, stickers and documents are kept in the chat to play or
download.

### One person, one contact

WhatsApp sometimes delivers a reply under a private id instead of the person's number. When APX already knows
which number that id belongs to (for example, because your agent wrote to them first), the reply goes to the same
contact and chat. If an earlier reply created a second contact, APX merges the two the next time WhatsApp connects:
one contact keeps both addresses, the details you set and both chats' messages in order. If you had set up both
contacts, the joined one keeps the stricter choice of each: silenced or supervised if either was, blocked if either
was, answered at any hour only if both were, and only the permissions both had.

### Files that never arrived

A file that could not be downloaded when it came (or that arrived before APX read media) shows as a note such as
"[video — APX cannot read this kind of message yet]". Press **Recover files** in that chat, or run
`apx channels recover <thread>`: APX asks your phone to send those messages again and puts each file in its message,
without a new answer from the agent. Your phone has to be online for it to answer.

## Groups

Your agent can also look after WhatsApp groups, the way a Telegram bot looks after its groups. Groups are opt-in: one
that isn't listed doesn't exist for APX, so nothing of it is read or stored, even with the line in it.

<Steps>

1. Add the line to the group on WhatsApp (or, on your own number, be in it).

2. In **Channels**, open the account's settings and go to **Groups**. While WhatsApp is connected, pick the group from
   **Add a group this line is in…**. A group where only admins can write says the line cannot write there.

3. Choose its mode, and **Save**.

</Steps>

Each group has the same modes, words that call it, notes, rules and limits as a Discord room (see
[Server rooms](/channels/discord-slack/#server-rooms)). Everything said in a listed group is kept in that group's chat
in APX, so you and your agents can read it. In **When called**, an @mention of the line, a reply to one of its
messages or one of the words that call it gets an answer. Answers go to the group, quoting the message they answer.
Photos, files and voice notes in a group are noted by kind; they are not downloaded or transcribed.

<Aside type="caution" title="Groups are public, so their turns are sealed">
In a group the agent answers with no memory and nothing of yours, even when you're the one writing. Private chats
don't change.
</Aside>

### Groups on your own number

When **This line is my own phone** is on, anything the agent writes in a group comes from your number, and the group
reads it as you. So your groups are **only read** at first: what is said is kept, and the agent never answers there,
whatever mode a group has. To let it answer, turn on **Answer in groups as me** in **Groups**; each group's mode applies
from then on. Until you do, an agent can't post in those groups for you either.

On your own number, an @mention of you or a reply to your message is meant for you, not the agent: only the words that
call it (under **What calls it**) do. What you type in a group from your phone is kept as yours.

## Phone numbers

WhatsApp needs every number with its country code. A number saved the local way (`976 666 004` in Peru, `11 2345 6789`
in Argentina) is completed with your own line's country code when you or your agent save it, and again before each
message goes out. Argentine mobiles go as `+54 9`, as WhatsApp expects them.

Before writing to a number, APX checks that it is on WhatsApp. When a number is not on WhatsApp, or can't be made
into a valid one (its country is not yours and it was saved without its code), nothing is sent: your agent is told it
did not go out, and why. Save that contact's number with its country code (`+51 976 666 004`) and ask again.

A contact saved before without the country code keeps working: the first message to them fixes their number, and
their answer comes back to the same chat.

## Strangers

Strangers work as on every channel (**Send a code**, **Ask me**, **Answer**, **Ignore**), with one difference on your
own number: APX never sends a code from it. There, the first option is **List them**: the person is listed in the
channel's settings for you to approve, and nothing is sent to them.

**Ask me** is often the most comfortable on WhatsApp: you get a notice with who wrote, their number and their first
message. Say yes (the button, or tell your agent) and that first message is answered.

## When it disconnects

- If WhatsApp logs the device out (you removed it from **Linked devices**, for example), APX deletes the stored
  session and the channel shows an error. Press **Link again** and scan a new code.
- **Unlink WhatsApp** removes the channel and its session folder.

<Aside type="caution">
APX uses WhatsApp's linked-device protocol through an open-source library, not an official API. Use it for normal
conversations, not bulk messaging.
</Aside>
